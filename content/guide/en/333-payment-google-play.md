# Google Play Store payments

> This article is provided by **CardNav [cardnav.xyz](https://cardnav.xyz)**.

Android users can subscribe inside official AI apps through Google Play. The store processes the order, without requiring a card directly on the AI website. Regional eligibility and payment checks still apply, so approval is not guaranteed.

## Before paying

- Prepare a Google Account eligible to purchase in the intended Play region, a device with working Google Play, and an official app offering the intended plan through in-app purchases.
- Prepare a bank card supported in that region and accurate payment details. Check for an existing website or Apple subscription to avoid duplicates.
- Ensure the store and app are reachable; see [Network access and VPNs](./321-tool-vpn.md).

## Subscribe step by step

1. **Add a payment method**: In Google Play, tap your profile picture → “Payments & subscriptions” → “Payment methods.” Add a supported bank card and complete any verification.
2. **Download the official app**: Confirm the Google Account currently used by the store. Find the official AI app, check its developer and download it.
3. **Sign in to the AI account**: Open the app and sign in to the account you want to upgrade. Confirm the email address.
4. **Pay inside the app**: Select a plan. In the Google Play purchase dialog, check the amount, billing period and purchasing account, then pay with the added card.
5. **Verify activation**: Check the app’s benefits and Google Play order. If charged without activation, verify both purchasing and AI accounts and contact support with the order instead of paying again.

## Renewals and cancellation

Open Google Play → profile picture → “Payments & subscriptions” → “Subscriptions” to manage renewals or cancel. Cancellation is not a refund. If payment fails, use the error message to check the card, payment details or bank restrictions.

Play credit, web subscriptions and API credit are not automatically interchangeable. For charges, duplicate subscriptions and cancellation, see [ChatGPT subscriptions and top-ups](./334-chatgpt-subscription-payment.md).
