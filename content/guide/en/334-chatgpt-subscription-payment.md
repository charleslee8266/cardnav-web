# ChatGPT subscriptions and top-ups

> This article is provided by **CardNav [cardnav.xyz](https://cardnav.xyz)**.

Before subscribing to ChatGPT, verify the signed-in account, plan entitlements and payment channel. ChatGPT subscriptions and API usage are billed separately; API balance cannot activate a ChatGPT subscription.

When you sign in to Codex with ChatGPT, you use the Codex entitlements provided by that plan. With an API key, check the corresponding API bill. Confirm your sign-in method before buying: API credit is not a ChatGPT subscription renewal.

## Plans and prices

Compare features, usage allowances and billing periods for plans such as Plus and Pro against your needs. Entitlements and eligibility may change; follow the plan details in your account and at checkout. Use the [official subscription price comparison](https://cardnav.xyz/official-price) for regional prices, checking currency, taxes and the update date.

For third-party offers, also verify ownership, validity, delivery contents and support coverage. Team seats, shared accounts and accounts with only partial validity remaining can differ from personal subscriptions in permissions and duration. Do not compare sticker prices alone. See [Buying and signing in to ready-made accounts](./212-usage-ready-account.md) for purchase checks.

## Payment channels

Website and in-app purchases have different payment, renewal and support entry points. Check that your chosen channel supports the plan and that no unexpired subscription already exists before buying again.

| Purchase channel | Check before payment |
| --- | --- |
| ChatGPT website | Signed-in account, plan, billing details and checkout payment options |
| Apple App Store | Official app, Apple account region, available plan and payment method |
| Google Play | Official app, Google account region, billing details and available plans |
| Third-party top-up | Target account, duration, payment method and failure or missing-delivery policy |

For app-store purchases, see [App Store payments](./332-payment-app-store.md) and [Google Play Store payments](./333-payment-google-play.md). Before buying a gift card, check region, redemption conditions and eligible uses. Redeemable store credit is not necessarily usable for the subscription.

With third-party top-ups, retain control of email, recovery methods and multi-factor authentication. Passwords, verification codes and credentials such as cookies or Session data may grant account access; do not publish them or submit them to unknown sites. See [Third-party top-ups](./213-usage-third-party-top-up.md) for delivery and support checks.

## Payment troubleshooting

| Problem | Action |
| --- | --- |
| Card declined; payment incomplete | Check billing details, card status and issuer restrictions; contact the bank or official support according to the error |
| Bank shows pending; subscription inactive | Distinguish a temporary authorization from a posted charge; save the time and record and avoid repeated payments |
| Charged but plan unchanged | Check account, workspace and purchase channel; contact that channel's support with the order |
| Charges on both website and mobile | Review website, Apple and Google subscription records for duplicate subscriptions |
| Seller received payment but did not activate | Request resolution under the order terms; recover seller payments through the original transaction channel |

After a decline, also check whether the account and payment method meet official regional and usage requirements. Changing IP, buying gift cards or adding funds cannot guarantee a solution.

## Renewals, cancellation and refunds

Cancellation, refunds and account deletion address different matters:

- **Cancel renewal**: Stop future automatic charges. Effective date and remaining access follow the subscription page.
- **Request a refund**: Address an already-paid order. Eligibility, amount and procedure depend on the purchase channel.
- **Delete the account**: Affects data and access rights; it is not a routine subscription-management method.

Manage website purchases in ChatGPT account settings and Apple or Google purchases in their stores. When canceling or requesting a refund, retain the plan name, renewal date, order number and outcome, and check for active subscriptions in other channels.
