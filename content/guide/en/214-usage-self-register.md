# 2.4 Official subscriptions

> This article is provided by **CardNav [cardnav.xyz](https://cardnav.xyz)**.

Registering and subscribing through official channels gives you control of the account, payment records and recovery methods. For long-term use, this simplifies renewals and support and reduces reliance on seller-provided accounts or third-party payment assistance.

## Main advantages

- **Manage your own account**: Maintain email, recovery methods and security settings over time.
- **Clear billing records**: Pay with your own method, keep orders and request support through the relevant channel.
- **Suitable for long-term use**: Avoid moving data because a seller changes account delivery or team seats.

## Limitations and risks

- **Eligibility requirements**: Supported regions, verification and payment channels vary by platform.
- **Plans have limits**: A subscription does not mean unlimited usage, an API balance or access to every feature.
- **Account security still matters**: Unauthorized access, rule violations and payment issues may affect service. Registering yourself cannot guarantee permanent access.

## Who it suits

- Long-term users of official websites or clients
- Users who need control of their data, bills and account recovery
- Users who do not want renewals and account access to depend on a seller

Where required, prepare a phone number that can continue receiving verification messages. Disposable numbers make long-term account recovery harder; see [Overseas phone verification](./324-tool-phone-verification.md).

## Payment methods
<!-- badge="Preparation" icon="card" -->

Choose an officially supported payment method for the product and region. Check currency, taxes, automatic renewal and the refund channel.

See [International payments and price differences](./330-payment-overview.md), [App Store payments](./332-payment-app-store.md) and [Google Play Store payments](./333-payment-google-play.md).

## Setup steps

1. Confirm supported regions, registration requirements and the intended plan through the official service. Distinguish web subscriptions from API billing.
2. Register with an email address you can manage long term, complete required verification and provide accurate information.
3. Configure supported two-factor authentication and recovery methods, and store recovery information securely.
4. Confirm that the payment channel supports the plan. Check the final amount, renewal cycle and cancellation procedure before paying.
5. Verify the plan, features and renewal date in your account, and keep the order record.
6. Monitor login alerts and bills. Use the relevant official channel for unfamiliar devices, duplicate charges or missing benefits.

## Recommendations

Before subscribing, use real tasks to assess whether free features or a short subscription meet your needs. For ongoing use, review renewal costs and usage frequency regularly, and retain copies of important materials.
