# Choosing a reliable seller

> This article is provided by **CardNav [cardnav.xyz](https://cardnav.xyz)**.

When buying AI accounts, subscriptions or other digital goods, consider whether you can reach the seller and receive the agreed support if something goes wrong. Prefer sellers with platform dispute channels, responsive public support groups, ongoing operations, and clear product and support terms.

## 1. Consider platform sellers first
<!-- badge="Indicator 1" icon="support" -->

Consider sellers on platforms such as 链动小铺, 16688 and 云猫寄售. Platform sellers usually follow shared transaction rules, order records and delivery processes. If a transaction issue arises, you can file a complaint through their platform if needed.

- **Complaints and appeals**: Check whether the platform offers a dispute channel, when it can intervene and how complaints are handled.
- **Orders and receipts**: Place orders and pay within the platform where possible. Save order records to help verify transactions and request support.
- **Scope of protection**: Refunds, buyer protection and compensation depend on the platform rules and product terms. Platform membership alone does not guarantee a safe transaction.

If a seller asks you to move to private messages and pay outside the platform, check whether your transaction records and dispute rights remain covered. For self-hosted stores, examine operating history, contact channels and support capacity more carefully.

## 2. Check support groups and promotional activity
<!-- badge="Indicator 2" icon="globe" -->

Before buying, consider how actively the seller promotes their business and engages in support group chats. Favor sellers with public group chats and timely replies. Groups let you see other users’ questions and how the seller actually responds and resolves issues.

- **Recent, genuine conversations**: Look for discussions about purchases, usage, restocking and support. Group size or advertising volume alone is insufficient.
- **Replies and resolutions**: Check whether questions receive answers and replacement or refund requests receive follow-up. Friendly pre-sales replies alone are insufficient.
- **Ongoing promotion and updates**: Product, campaign and announcement updates on official X accounts, Telegram channels and similar services can indicate continued investment in the business.
- **Stable contact channels**: In addition to group chats, check for a working support account or ticket system. Save contact details and order information.

An announcement-only channel does not offer the same view of user feedback as an open discussion group. Activity alone does not prove reliability. Long periods without updates, unanswered questions and unresolved support issues all deserve caution.

## 3. Check continuity of operations
<!-- badge="Indicator 3" icon="toolbox" -->

Long-established sellers that remain active usually offer more history to assess. Compare these dates:

- **Platform join date**: When the seller opened a store on that platform.
- **Domain registration date**: A clue to the domain’s history. Domain changes or acquired old domains require other supporting records.
- **CardNav listing date**: When CardNav added the seller, not when the business was founded.

Combine these dates with recent activity: updated product descriptions, announcements, group replies and restocking. An old domain or long-established store may still be abandoned, so age alone is insufficient.

## 4. Check product fit and consistent supply
<!-- badge="Indicator 4" icon="cart" -->

A broad catalog that keeps up with popular AI products can indicate maintained supply. Focus on whether the product you need is clearly described and consistently available.

- **Needs and benefits**: Verify the model or plan, subscription duration, private or shared access, account ownership, login method and delivery contents. Similar product names can describe different services.
- **Catalog quality**: A maintained catalog makes comparison easier. Duplicate listings, outdated descriptions and low-quality offers provide little evidence of operating capability.
- **Stock and restocking**: Regular availability and replenishment suggest active supply management. Be cautious if many products remain unavailable without updates.
- **Sales and stock changes**: Seller-reported sales can be useful. CardNav’s daily and 30-day stock decreases reflect merchant activity, but may include manual adjustments or reseller stock synchronization and do not equal actual sales.

For sellers with few products, check their investment in the business and capacity to handle support: can they continue resolving widespread account bans or refund requests? A large catalog does not replace these checks either.

## 5. Check payment methods and support terms
<!-- badge="Indicator 5" icon="cash" -->

Before paying, confirm support coverage and contact channels. Prefer payment methods that preserve complete transaction records.

- **Coverage and replacements**: When does coverage begin, how long does it last, and what happens if login fails, benefits expire or an account is banned?
- **Refunds and restrictions**: Which situations qualify for refunds or replacements? Check evidence requirements, usage conditions and claim deadlines.
- **Recipient and payment records**: Fiat channels such as Alipay and WeChat Pay usually provide recipient details and payment receipts. Verify the recipient; the payment method alone does not prove safety.
- **Cryptocurrency payments**: Funds are usually difficult to recover if the seller disappears or commits fraud. Be cautious with sellers accepting only cryptocurrency and offering no other credible operating history.

Save product descriptions, orders, payment receipts and key conversations. Ask for clarification when an offer promises “support included” or “guaranteed stability” without specific resolution terms.

## 6. Compare equivalent offers and start small
<!-- badge="Indicator 6" icon="check" -->

Compare the same plan, duration, account type and support coverage. High prices may carry a substantial markup; prices far below comparable offers warrant checking for reduced benefits, unclear origins or limited support. Do not choose solely by the lowest price.

For a first purchase, start small to check delivery, login, promised benefits and support responsiveness. One successful delivery is only a reference point. For longer use, continue assessing operating history and support performance, and avoid large initial deposits or bulk purchases.
