# App Store payments

> This article is provided by **CardNav [cardnav.xyz](https://cardnav.xyz)**.

App Store purchases are billed by Apple, so you do not need to add a bank card directly to the AI website. Redeem a gift card valid for your account region and intended subscription, then pay inside the official AI app with Apple Account credit. Store and app requirements for region, account and payment still apply.

## Before paying

- Prepare an Apple Account for purchases in the intended App Store region, with accurate payment details and access to the official app and plan.
- The gift card must match the account region, redeem into account credit and support the intended subscription. Confirm this before buying.
- Make sure the store and app are reachable; see [Network access and VPNs](./321-tool-vpn.md). Check for an existing subscription through another channel to avoid paying twice.

## Subscribe step by step

1. **Sign in and download**: Sign in to the App Store with the Apple Account used for purchases. Find the official AI app, check its developer and download it.
2. **Redeem the gift card**: Open the App Store, tap your profile picture, then “Redeem Gift Card or Code.” Scan or enter the code and check that your Apple Account balance has increased.
3. **Sign in to the AI account**: Open the app and sign in to the account you want to upgrade. Confirm the email address.
4. **Purchase in the app**: Select a plan, check the amount and billing period, and confirm the Apple purchase. Account credit pays when eligible; add funds or a backup payment method if the balance is insufficient or the system requests one.
5. **Verify activation**: Check the app’s plan status and Apple order. If charged without activation, do not repurchase. Try “Restore Purchases” if the app offers it, or contact support with the order.

## Renewals and cancellation

On iPhone, open “Settings → your name → Subscriptions” to view renewal dates or cancel. Cancellation is not a refund; request refunds separately under Apple’s order rules.

Redeemed gift cards are subject to balance and refund restrictions.
