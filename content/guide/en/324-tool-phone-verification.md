# Overseas Phone Verification

> This article is provided by **CardNav [cardnav.xyz](https://cardnav.xyz)**.

Check the registration or verification page to learn whether an AI service requires a phone number and which regions and number types it accepts. Before obtaining a number, consider whether future codes must arrive at that same number. Long-term paid accounts should use verification methods you can continue to manage.

## Temporary SMS Numbers
<!-- badge="Short-term use" icon="phone" -->

Temporary SMS numbers suit quick trials and short-term verification without ongoing number upkeep. Reuse is uncertain, making them unsuitable for long-term paid AI accounts that rely on the original number for recovery.

### Before use
- Number regions and types accepted by the platform
- Validity, repeat SMS access, and refunds for failed delivery
- Other recovery options after the number expires

### HeroSMS

HeroSMS lets you choose temporary SMS numbers by platform and region for short-term registration checks. Charges, replacements and refunds follow current terms; receiving one code does not ensure ongoing account recovery.

## Keeping a Long-Term Number
<!-- badge="Long-term use" icon="key" -->

A number you retain supports later verification and recovery for paid AI accounts and can serve several services. Physical SIMs still need platform acceptance and SMS reception in your location.

### Maintenance
- Check SIM issuance, activation, roaming, and SMS conditions
- Renew or maintain required activity to avoid number reassignment
- Keep the SIM and carrier account secure, and track validity
- Set up other verification and recovery methods where supported

### GiffGaff

GiffGaff’s UK SIM suits users who want to retain their own number for ongoing AI account verification. Check delivery, activation, roaming SMS and retention costs; no monthly fee does not mean free number retention.
