# ChatGPT Account Appeals

> This article is provided by **CardNav [cardnav.xyz](https://cardnav.xyz)**.

If you believe your ChatGPT account was deactivated by mistake, you can ask OpenAI to review it. The appropriate channel depends on whether you received and can access the notification email.

## Choose an Official Channel

1. **You received the notification**: Submit through the appeal link in the email so the request can be matched to the account and case.
2. **You cannot access that email**: Use the [official OpenAI appeal form](https://openai.com/form/appeal/).
3. **You received no notification but lost access**: Open the [OpenAI Help Center](https://help.openai.com/) and click the chat bubble at the bottom right. A virtual assistant responds first; unresolved issues may be passed to a human agent.

## Prepare the Details

- **Account information**: Sign-in email, login method, and plan. Official appeal guidance also lists User ID and Org ID, the user and organization identifiers.
- **What happened**: The exact deactivation message, date and time zone, actual use, and relevant context for requesting review.
- **Actions taken**: Describe troubleshooting and remedial steps. If you suspect compromise, include the possible date range. Official guidance recommends changing your password and rotating API keys.
- **Unauthorized charges**: Only if applicable, include dates, amounts, the card’s last four digits, card brand, issuing bank, and billing ZIP code.

Provide accurate information. Remove unrelated personal details from screenshots and attachments; do not share passwords or one-time codes.

## Address the Reason for the Restriction

Security concerns may lead to a protective suspension; incomplete identity or age verification may also cause deactivation. Follow official verification instructions when requested. If an identity-verification email is missing, ask support to resend it. The same appeal statement is not appropriate for every situation.

After submission, provide additional information as requested. The guidance gives no fixed processing time or guarantee of account restoration.
