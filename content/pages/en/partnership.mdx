# Business partnership

CardNav helps users find AI accounts, digital goods shops, API gateways, and related services. Users compare information before visiting a merchant site.

## Partnership options

<TwoColumn class="partnership-grid">
<Card tone="primary">

### Option 1: Affiliate partnership

For gateways and shops with an Affiliate system.

1. The merchant provides an Aff link, or CardNav registers to obtain one.
2. CardNav displays the Aff link (fixed placement is not guaranteed).
3. Users click the Aff link to enter and use the merchant site.
4. The merchant pays a commission on completed sales; 5%~20% is recommended.

</Card>

<Card tone="teal">

### Option 2: Small contributions

For users and merchants who want to support CardNav's maintenance.

1. Users and merchants can [make a small contribution](/en/supporters?support-dialog) directly.
2. Confirmed contributions appear on the [supporters list](/en/supporters).
3. Merchants can link contributions to their [API gateway](/en/llm-gateway) or [shop](/en/shops) for a chance to appear in pinned placements. See the rules below.

</Card>

<Card tone="gold">

### Option 3: Fixed partnership

For merchants seeking more exposure.

1. The merchant provides the required information.
2. The merchant pays a monthly partnership fee; [contact us privately](https://t.me/cardnav_xyz_group) for details.
3. CardNav displays the merchant in fixed placements.
4. CardNav can provide click data.

</Card>
</TwoColumn>

## Placement rules

<TwoColumn class="partnership-details">
<DetailPanel>

### Contributing merchant placement rules

1. **Placement**: when filters match, merchants or selected products are pinned on the [gateway page](/en/llm-gateway) and [shop page](/en/shops), with a “Supporter” label.
2. **Limits**: the contribution section shows up to 10 pinned entries, with up to 2 entries per merchant.
3. **Current order**: merchants are ordered by total contributions, from highest to lowest.
4. **Future adjustments**: if more merchants contribute, each contribution will be weighted to decrease over time. These values will be added into a contribution score, with higher scores appearing first.

</DetailPanel>

<DetailPanel>

### Fixed partnership placement rules

1. **Sponsor list**: displayed across all pages. **Merchants must provide an image with a 3:1 aspect ratio**.
2. **Pinned table entries**: when filters match, merchants or selected products are pinned on the [gateway page](/en/llm-gateway) and [shop page](/en/shops), with a “Partner” label.
3. **Official group**: promotional links can be posted and pinned in the [official Telegram group](https://t.me/cardnav_xyz_group).
4. **Other requests**: see an existing merchant, such as `GeniusCoder`, for an example. Other requirements can be [discussed in detail](https://t.me/cardnav_xyz_group).

</DetailPanel>
</TwoColumn>

## Partnership requirements and user judgment

<TwoColumn class="partnership-columns">
<DetailPanel>

### Partnership requirements

1. The website is accessible and its product information is clear and accurate
2. A support group or channel is available for CardNav to observe and remains usable
3. No phishing, impersonation, malicious redirects, fraud, or other high-risk content
4. The partnership must not be hidden or presented as natural ranking

</DetailPanel>

<DetailPanel>

### User judgment

1. Partnership means CardNav has completed an initial review; it does not guarantee operation, fulfillment, or after-sales service
2. Users should independently verify the domain, products, prices, payment, support, and after-sales service
3. Deceptive, malicious, or high-risk content may be removed, and the partnership may be suspended or ended

</DetailPanel>
</TwoColumn>
