# Business partnership

CardNav helps users find AI accounts, digital goods shops, API gateways, and related services. Users search products, compare prices and stock, review merchant information, and then visit the merchant site.

## Partnership options

### Affiliate partnership

This option is for API gateways and merchants with an Affiliate or referral system. Shops may also use this option when they can provide a trackable Affiliate link.

- The merchant provides a trackable Affiliate link.
- CardNav uses that link as the partnership destination.
- Commission terms, attribution, and settlement timing are agreed by both sides.

Affiliate-only partnerships do not guarantee a fixed placement. Placement depends on relevance, user experience, and partnership performance.

### Fixed merchant partnership

Partner merchants receive a clear partnership label and agreed exposure, including search-result placement, the partner merchant area, optional coupon links, and click reporting where available.

Partner products are labeled “Partner” and appear below user favorites. Up to 10 partner products can be pinned in total and up to 5 products can be pinned for one merchant. When multiple partner merchants match a search, positions are distributed as evenly as possible. A merchant is not guaranteed to appear when its products do not match the search.

## Review and user judgment

“Partner” means that CardNav has completed an initial review of the merchant. It does not guarantee the merchant’s operation, fulfillment, or after-sales service. Users should verify the domain, product details, prices, payment methods, support channels, and after-sales terms themselves.

Merchants involved in deception, phishing, impersonation, malicious redirects, or clearly high-risk activity will be removed immediately. CardNav may suspend or end the partnership.

## Requirements

- The website is accessible and clearly presents products, prices, and purchase links.
- A Telegram support group or channel must be available for CardNav to observe.
- Support channels must remain available during the partnership.
- Product information, coupons, payment methods, and after-sales terms must be accurate and verifiable.
- Partners must not ask CardNav to hide the partnership or disguise natural ranking.
