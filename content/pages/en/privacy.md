# Privacy Policy

CardNav values user privacy. This page explains what information we may collect on the public site, how it may be used, and what to keep in mind when visiting third-party links.

## Information We May Collect

When you visit CardNav, servers and analytics tools may record basic access data such as visit time, page path, referrer, device and browser information, and rough IP network information. This helps us understand page visits, troubleshoot issues, and improve the site experience.

We store a randomly generated visitor ID in your browser to deduplicate product impressions and clicks, gateway detail visits and outbound opens, and model detail visits by day. The ID contains no name or IP address, but can link visits and favorites from the same browser. It is regenerated if you clear this site's data. Server-side visit deduplication records are normally kept for about 3 days; aggregate counts help us understand usage and improve content presentation.

When you click a product or merchant outbound link, we also record random visitor and session identifiers, the click time, applied search terms and filters, the destination merchant and product, list position, partnership or support labels, and whether a safety notice appeared for that navigation. These records help us assess search results and compare merchant click distribution. Cancelling the notice does not create an outbound click record. Please do not enter passwords or other sensitive information in search fields.

Your product and shop merchant favorites are stored in your browser. On your first visit each day and when you add or remove a favorite, CardNav automatically syncs your random visitor ID, the identifiers of currently saved products and merchants (older favorites may include names), and the sync time. We use this information to understand aggregate preferences and improve content presentation and recommendations. The server retains only your latest list, without daily copies; successfully syncing a removal or an empty list replaces the corresponding records. Records without a successful sync for 30 consecutive days are removed. Your browser keeps your favorites until you remove them or clear this site's data. Clearing browser data does not immediately delete previously uploaded records; those expire as described above, or you can contact us to request deletion.

When you submit a merchant site URL or contact us through Telegram, we receive the URL, message content, and account information shown by the corresponding platform that you voluntarily provide. This information is used to process submissions, communicate about partnerships, and maintain site content.

When contributing, you may provide a nickname, email and message, or select a listed merchant or gateway. Nicknames, messages, selected sites and total confirmed amounts appear on the supporters list; a blank nickname is shown as anonymous. Email is used to confirm order issues and for necessary follow-up communication. It is not publicly displayed or provided to third parties. Epay processes payments, while CardNav stores order amounts, payment status and related information needed to confirm receipt.

## How We Use Information

We use collected information to maintain site operation, analyze visit trends, improve search and page experience, process merchant submissions, identify spam submissions or abnormal access, and respond to users or merchants.

## Third-Party Services And Links

CardNav pages may include third-party merchant links, Telegram links, GitHub links, website analytics scripts, or advertising and partnership content. When you visit a third-party website, that third party's own privacy policy and terms of service apply. CardNav cannot control how third parties collect or use your information.

## Information Sharing

Unless required to process user requests, comply with laws and regulations, investigate security issues, or maintain normal site operation, we do not actively sell or publicly disclose users' personal information. Merchant information shown on public pages mainly comes from publicly accessible merchant pages or information submitted by merchants.

## Data Retention And Security

We retain relevant information for a reasonable period needed to fulfill the purposes above and take reasonable measures to reduce the risk of unauthorized access, leakage, or misuse. However, internet transmission and communication through third-party platforms cannot be guaranteed to be absolutely secure.

## Contact Us

If you want to correct or delete relevant information, or if you have questions about this privacy policy, you can contact us through the Telegram entry at the top of the page.
